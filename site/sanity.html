Legendaries

In Spin mode, each spin has a 1.5% chance to be a Legend spin, which offers 10 random legendaries to pick from. In Packs, each pack has a 1.5% chance of a bonus legendary card. There is no limit per run. Sorted by their best spot.

Legendaries are left out of the counts and top/bottom lists above because they only appear in Legend spins and as bonus pack cards.
